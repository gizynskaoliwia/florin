● The variable-income reality

Most budget apps assume a steady paycheck. Yours doesn't.

When you're a freelancer or sole proprietor, every month is different. You owe VAT to one place, income tax to another, ZUS to a third — and what's left changes constantly. Then you're supposed to budget on top of that.

Florin starts where the chaos starts: at the gross invoice. It walks the deductions with you, hands you the real net number, and only then asks how you'd like to split it.

"I'd get an invoice, pay myself, and three weeks later realise I was short on ZUS again." — common freelance month
"Spreadsheets work until they don't. I want something that opens, calmly, and tells me where I stand." — sole proprietor in Warsaw
"I don't want my money in a SaaS dashboard. I want it on my laptop, and that's it." — privacy by default

● Privacy by architecture

Your money stays on your machine.

Florin is a single SQLite file on your laptop. There's no account to create, no server to log into, no cloud backup we secretly have. If you delete the app, your data is gone — and so is anyone else's chance of seeing it.

●

Local-only by default

One file: ~/Library/Application Support/Florin/florin.db on macOS, the Windows equivalent on Windows.

●

No telemetry, no tracking

Florin doesn't phone home. The app makes no network calls — you can verify this in Activity Monitor or with Little Snitch.

●

Backup is your choice

Copy the .db file to a USB drive, your own iCloud, or whatever you trust. Florin will not pick a cloud provider for you.

●

Open source — read it yourself

MIT-licensed Python. The whole codebase is small enough to read in an afternoon.
