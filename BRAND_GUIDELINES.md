# Brand Guidelines: Florin

## 1. App Identity & Purpose

**Name:** Florin

**Meaning:** The name refers to the florin — a historic gold coin minted in Florence starting in 1252.

**Purpose:** Florin is a desktop personal finance application designed for comprehensive expense tracking, income management, and savings planning. It provides users with a clear overview of their monthly budget, cash flow buffer calculations, and historical financial summaries.

## 2. Visual Style & Vibe

*   **Minimalism:** The design should be simple and minimalistic, avoiding clutter and focusing on essential information.
*   **Color Palette:** The color palette should be calm and peaceful (*spokojna kolorystyka*). This creates a stress-free, approachable environment for managing finances.
*   **Contrast & UX:** While the overall tone is muted and calm, specific interactive or important elements (e.g., net income amounts, expenses, call-to-action buttons, warnings) should stand out clearly to ensure good user experience (UX) and high readability.
